🌍 How Your Emissions Affect the Environment

Your carbon footprint may seem small when viewed individually, but when millions of households produce similar emissions, the combined effect becomes significant.

🌡️

Global Warming

Carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere. Large amounts of these emissions contribute to rising global temperatures.

🌧️

Changing Climate

Increasing greenhouse-gas emissions can contribute to changes in rainfall, weather patterns and the frequency of some extreme weather events.

🌳

Ecosystems

Climate change can put pressure on forests, wildlife, agriculture and other ecosystems that depend on stable environmental conditions.

🌊

Sea Level Rise

Long-term global warming contributes to melting land ice and expansion of warmer seawater, which contributes to rising sea levels.
